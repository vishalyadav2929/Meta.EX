# Lead tracking: who actually became a client

## Hard truth
Meta never shares who visited a profile or clicked a link. Profile-visit and link-click ads cannot be traced to people. Only these can: Messages ads (the chat is tagged with the ad), Lead forms (you get the form), or your own tracking.

## Recover past leads (manual, 1-2 hours)
1. Instagram app → DMs: scroll back through the date ranges when the top ads ran (the "Ends" column in the export) and note every enquiry.
2. Meta Business Suite → Inbox: chats that started from an ad carry an ad label. Filter by the "Ads" label if it's shown.
3. Comments on the ad posts: tags and "price?" comments count as leads.
4. The phone's WhatsApp: search for "anchor", "date", "booking" around the same dates.
Log everything in the tracker sheet.

## Track from now on
- **Messages objective** for every ad.
- **One DM keyword per ad** ("DM BARAAT", "DM SANGEET"). An auto-reply tool (ManyChat or similar) can tag the source automatically.
- **Ask every enquiry:** "Aapne hume kahan dekha?"
- **Tracker sheet** columns: Date, Name, Phone, Event date, City, Source ad/keyword, Status (New/Talking/Booked/Lost), Booking ₹, Notes. Generate it with `scripts/make_lead_tracker.py`.
- Weekly: leads per ad, bookings per ad, cost per booking = ad spend ÷ bookings.
