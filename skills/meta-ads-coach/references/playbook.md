# Playbook: leads for a local service business on ₹300-500/day

## 1. Objective
- **Engagement → Messages** (Instagram DM / WhatsApp / Messenger). The result is "Messaging conversations started", and every chat appears in the Business Suite inbox tagged with the ad.
- Or **Leads → Instant form** (name, phone, event date, city). Gives contact details directly. Add a qualifying question (event date) to cut junk.
- Avoid boosting for "Link clicks" or "Profile visits" when the goal is bookings. These objectives buy cheap taps, not enquiries.

## 2. Structure (small budgets)
- 1 campaign → 1 ad set → 2-3 ads (different hooks, same offer). Splitting ₹300/day across many ad sets starves each of them.
- The first 48 hours are the learning phase: don't edit or judge.

## 3. Audience
1. **Warm (cheapest):** people who engaged with the IG account in the last 365 days, viewers of 50%+ of a video, people who messaged before. Create them in Audiences → Custom audience → Instagram account.
2. **Lookalike 1%** of the warm audience, in the home state.
3. **Broad local:** home city + 25-40 km, age 24-45, Advantage+ audience on.
Put most of the budget on warm when it has 1,000+ people.

## 4. Creative
- The first 2 seconds show the best crowd or energy moment (for an anchor: baraat or a crowd game).
- The middle shows proof: a couple's or parent's testimonial, the number of weddings done, cities covered.
- The end has one CTA: "DM 'BOOK' for your date" / "Check availability".
- Reuse the themes that won in the export. Refresh a creative when frequency goes above 3.

## 5. Budget maths
```
leads ≈ budget ÷ cost per lead (CPL)
budget needed = target leads × CPL
```
- Use the account's own messaging CPL once it exists.
- No history yet → [Guessing] ₹30-100 per conversation for Indian wedding/event services. Run a ₹1,500-2,100 test first.
- The real metric is cost per booking = spend ÷ bookings. Ask for the booking value and the close rate, then set target CPL = (booking value × acceptable ad share) ÷ (leads needed per booking).

## 6. Guard rules (Ads Manager → Rules → Create rule, or the meta-ex cron)
| Rule | Action |
|---|---|
| Spend ≥ 3× daily budget and 0 results (3 days) | Pause ad |
| Cost per result > 2× target (3 days) | Pause ad |
| Frequency > 3 (7 days) | Notify: new creative |
| Daily spend > cap | Pause campaign |
| Ad < 48 h old | Ignore all rules |

## 7. Free leads first
Before spending: message everyone who enquired in the last 12-18 months ("2026-27 dates filling, aapki date free hai?"). Post a story with a DM keyword. These convert faster than any ad.
